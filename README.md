I’m working on a 2D space survival platformer where you crash in the middle of nowhere in the space. Your goal is to explore, blast mini alien monsters, and search for lost rocket parts to rebuild your ship and escape space.

This IS the 2nd game of my life, I have had alot of fun making it and spent alot of time on it and definately it turned out great

The game is now playable at: https://rafaybuildstuff.itch.io/escape-the-space

Please play the game and drop your honest feedback asw!

Quick Note: All the art is hand drawn and No AI is used

Controls: 
-arrow keys to move
-sppace to jump
-Press "E" to equip weapon
-Press "Q" to shoot
